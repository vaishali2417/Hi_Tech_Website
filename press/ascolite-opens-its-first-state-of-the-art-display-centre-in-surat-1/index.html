PRODUCT INFORMATION

Four Products for Essential Construction Applications

HI TECH offers masonry mortar, ready-mixed mortar, non-shrink grout and concrete floor hardener. Product pages provide application, coverage and storage information for each material.

ASCOFIX BJM/TBM

Pre-mixed mortar for block masonry.

READY MIX MORTAR

Ready-mixed mortar for walling and plaster applications.

ASCOGROUT 65

Non-shrink cementitious grout for construction applications.

ASCOTOP FH 111

Dry-shake floor hardener for concrete surfaces.

For project-specific product enquiries, contact HI TECH.
