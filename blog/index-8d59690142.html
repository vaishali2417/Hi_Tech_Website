Top Waterproofing Solutions for Residential and Commercial Buildings

September 9, 2024

Water intrusion can cause significant damage over time, particularly to foundations, walls, and roofs. Waterproofing helps prevent issues such as:

  • Cracks in the foundation
  • Mold and mildew growth
  • Corrosion of steel reinforcement
  • Peeling paint and wall damage
  • Structural weakening

In both residential and commercial waterproofing, using the right and best waterproofing solutions with right products can increase the lifespan of the building and save on future repairs.

Top Waterproofing Methods for Residential and Commercial Buildings

There are several effective waterproofing techniques, each suited to different parts of a building and specific requirements. Here’s a breakdown of some of the best methods available today.

Liquid Waterproofing Membranes

One of the most popular methods for both residential and commercial use, liquid waterproofing membranes offer flexibility and durability. These membranes are applied as a liquid and then solidify to form a seamless barrier against moisture.

  • Best For: Roofs, balconies, basements
  • Example Product: ASCOGUARD PRIME – A high-performance, fiber-enriched elastomeric membrane ideal for roof waterproofing, providing excellent flexibility and resistance to weather conditions.

Cementitious Waterproofing

A widely-used method due to its ease of application, cementitious waterproofing involves using a cement-based waterproofing mixture that forms a protective layer over surfaces. It’s often used in areas like basements or wet rooms.

  • Best For: Water tanks, basements, internal and external walls
  • Example Product: ASCOSEAL CL – A cement-based crystalline waterproofing coating that creates a durable, maintenance-free barrier by filling the pores in the concrete structure.

Bituminous Membrane Waterproofing

Bituminous membrane waterproofing is an excellent option for flat roofs and underground structures. It involves applying bitumen (a sticky, black substance) to create a waterproof seal. The sheets are often heated and fused to create a robust layer.

  • Best For: Flat roofs, basements, terraces
  • Example Product: Bitumen-based waterproofing membranes for heavy-duty commercial use.

Polyurethane Liquid Membrane

A polyurethane-based solution offers a seamless, flexible coating that is highly resistant to moisture and extreme temperatures. This is an ideal solution for roofs and areas exposed to harsh weather conditions.

  • Best For: Roofs, balconies, terraces
  • Example Product: Polyurethane-based membranes designed for long-term protection.

Crystalline Waterproofing

This method works by using a penetrative crystalline coating that reacts with the moisture and free lime in concrete to form crystals. These crystals block the pores and capillaries, making it impossible for water to pass through.

  • Best For: Foundations, basements, water storage tanks
  • Example Product: ASCOSEAL CL II – A two-component fast-setting crystalline coating designed for use on concrete structures in both residential and commercial settings.

Best Waterproofing Solutions for Residential Buildings

For homeowners, the focus is often on protecting basements, bathrooms, and roofs. These are the most vulnerable areas for water penetration.

  • Basement Waterproofing: Basements are prone to leaks due to their contact with the ground. Cementitious waterproofing and liquid membranes are common solutions. Products like ASCOFLEX II, a two-part flexible waterproofing membrane, work well for interior and exterior applications.
  • Bathroom Waterproofing: Bathrooms see constant moisture exposure, making waterproof membranes essential to prevent seepage. Epoxy waterproofing and liquid membranes are typically used here.
  • Roof Waterproofing: Residential roofs need a flexible waterproofing solution like ASCOGUARD PRIME, which offers elasticity and can withstand thermal movements, preventing leaks from extreme weather conditions.

Best Waterproofing Solutions for Commercial Buildings

Commercial buildings often require heavy-duty waterproofing to protect against harsher environments, larger areas, and higher traffic. Below are the best waterproofing methods for commercial properties:

  • Roof Waterproofing for Commercial Properties: Large, flat roofs are common in commercial buildings and often experience water pooling. Bituminous membranes or polyurethane liquid membranes offer robust, long-lasting protection.
  • Waterproofing in Parking Areas: For underground or exposed parking areas, use epoxy coatings that provide both waterproofing and resistance to wear and tear from vehicles.
  • Foundation Waterproofing for Skyscrapers or Large Buildings: Crystalline coatings like ASCOSEAL CL II are perfect for providing deep waterproofing at the foundation level, protecting against hydrostatic pressure and long-term water damage.

Tips for Choosing the Right Waterproofing Product

Selecting the right waterproofing product depends on factors such as the area to be waterproofed, environmental conditions, and whether the project is residential or commercial. Here are some tips to help:

  • Consider the environment: For areas prone to moisture, like basements and bathrooms, use products like cementitious coatings or epoxy waterproofing.
  • Surface preparation is key: Ensure that the surface is clean, dry, and free of cracks before applying any waterproofing material to ensure long-lasting results.
  • Consult experts: For larger projects like commercial buildings, it’s always wise to consult a professional to ensure you are choosing the most effective solution.

Conclusion: Protect Your Building with the Best Waterproofing Solutions

Waterproofing is essential to ensuring the longevity and durability of any building, whether it’s residential or commercial. From cementitious coatings and bituminous membranes to epoxy solutions, each method offers distinct advantages depending on the project’s needs. By choosing the right waterproofing method and products like ASCOGUARD PRIME, ASCOSEAL CL, or ASCOFLEX II, you can effectively protect your structure from water damage for years to come.
