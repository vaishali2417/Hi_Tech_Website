ASCOREPELL SX

ASCOREPELL SX represents a solvent-based silane siloxane water repellent cum sealer formulated for concrete, brick, block and masonry surfaces. On drying, it maintains the original appearance of surfaces and its non-film-forming nature enables the substrate to breathe freely. ASCOREPELL SX offers versatile waterproofing applications for both exterior and interior surfaces on a long-term basis.

  • Recommended application
  • coverage
  • shelf life

SUITABLE SUBSTRATES:

  • Walls: Exposed Brick, Stucco walls, Block, and RCC Work 
  • Tiles: Concrete Roof Tiles, Unglazed Wall Tiles and Mangalore tiles
  • Stones: Natural and Artificial Stones/Marbles
  • Protective Coatings: Waterproofing & Protection of Drywall And Cement-boards
  • Others: Facades, Inclined, Vertical Areas to Arrest Ingress of Moisture

80 - 100 ft²/l /coat (depending on surface porosity)

Ascorepell SX should be applied in 2 coats

Store above floor level in a cool dry place for up to 24 months.

Key features and benefits:

FAST DRYING AND LOW ODOUR

EXCELLENT PENETRATION

REDUCES EFFLORESCENCE

REDUCES DUST PENETRATION

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is ASCOREPELL SX?
ASCOREPELL SX is a solvent-based silane siloxane water repellent and sealer formulated for concrete, brick, block, and masonry surfaces.
What are the primary benefits of ASCOREPELL SX?
It offers long-term waterproofing, maintains the original appearance of surfaces, and allows the substrate to breathe due to its non-film-forming nature.
Can ASCOREPELL SX be used on both exterior and interior surfaces?
Yes, ASCOREPELL SX is suitable for waterproofing applications on both exterior and interior surfaces.
How does ASCOREPELL SX maintain the appearance of treated surfaces?
It maintains the original appearance by not forming a film, allowing the natural look of the substrate to remain visible.
What surfaces can ASCOREPELL SX be applied to?
It can be applied to concrete, brick, block, and masonry surfaces.
