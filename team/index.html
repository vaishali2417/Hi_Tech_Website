Mr. Jai Prakash HI TECH

Founder & Chairman

Mr. Jai Prakash HI TECH is a true visionary with over 50 years of experience. He began working at a very young age, driven by a genuine passion and entrepreneurial spirit. Throughout his career, he has set remarkable benchmarks in various industries. In textiles, his expertise spans trading, exports, weaving, texturizing, dyeing, and printing. Recognizing opportunities in the construction industry, he developed some of the finest residential high-rise buildings in Surat. Observing the rapid innovation in building materials, he identified significant opportunities for vertical integration.

Mr. Navin HI TECH

Managing Director

Mr. Navin HI TECH With 18 years of industrial experience, Mr. Navin HI TECH embodies the spirit of a new-generation entrepreneur. He seamlessly blends time-tested old-school tactics with modern management approaches. This unique combination has enabled him to successfully build and lead an enthusiastic team, united by a common goal. He believes in flexibility, the necessity of constant change, and a strong customer orientation. His extensive experience spans all verticals of the group, with a particular focus on the building materials division.

Mr. Kamlesh Sewani

Managing Director - Ascon Group

Mr. Kamlesh Sewani, the visionary Managing Director of Ascon Group, is a paragon of strategic leadership in the real estate industry. With over two decades of experience, Mr. Kamlesh Sewani has been the driving force behind Ascon's remarkable growth since joining in 2005. His astute business acumen has orchestrated the successful sale and delivery of 1,500+ apartments, a testament to his unparalleled expertise in sales and marketing.

Mrs. Geeta Sewani

Director - Ascon Group

Mrs. Geeta Sewani, our dynamic Director - Ascon Group since 2005, is the cornerstone of the company's visionary leadership. With her multifaceted expertise in management, marketing, design, and R&D, Mrs. Geeta Sewani has been instrumental in shaping Ascon's strategic direction and global presence. She is a rare blend of creativity and strategic acumen, steering a 30-year legacy of excellence into new realms of luxury living.




Mrs. Geeta Sewani is a master negotiator and relationship builder, she deftly manages high-stakes vendor partnerships while nurturing a customer-centric culture. Her true passion lies in crafting spaces that elevate lives, finding joy in translating dreams into tangible luxury.

Mr. Nitin Lalwani

Chief Operating Officer

Mr. Nitin Lalwani is one of the founding members, designated Chief Operating Officer, and the driving force behind one of the most innovative and successful Construction Material manufacturing company of India, HI TECH, founded in 2011.

Born into a family with a strong entrepreneurial spirit, Mr. Lalwani was exposed to business practices and management principles early on. His formative years were marked by curiosity and a drive to learn. A firm believer in education and learning, Mr. Lalwani pursued an MBA in Marketing & Finance, honing his skills and gaining the knowledge necessary to lead.

Mr. Lalwani’s entrepreneurial journey began with a clear vision to create a company that could deliver exceptional value to its customers, employees, and stakeholders, while also contributing positively to society.

As an entrepreneur, visionary, and strategic leader, he has built HI TECH, that has not only exceled in business but also set new standards in corporate values, sustainability, and ethical leadership.

As COO, Mr. Nitin Lalwani drives operational excellence, streamlines processes, and ensures alignment across departments, focusing on strategic growth, and efficiency, and fostering a culture of innovation and collaboration within the organization. With a strong workforce of 600+ Skilled professionals, 100 + innovative products, 500 Distributors, 4000 Dealer Coverage, and product presence in 3700 projects commanding Pan India market presence, Today, HI TECHstands amongst the leading Construction Material Supplier in India

Mr. Nitin Lalwani is an avid reader who believes in the power of knowledge and technology. He constantly seeks new insights to drive innovation, fostering growth and efficiency in every aspect of business.
