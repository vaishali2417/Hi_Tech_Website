ASCOGROUT®65

ASCOGROUT® 65 is a specially modified multipurpose, high-low, non-shrink & cementitious precision grout specially designed to maintain its strengths at various consistencies from fowable to trowelable. ASCOGROUT® 65 produces high strength, plastic expansion, excellent substrate adhesion & durability. The low water demand ensures high early strength.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is ASCOGROUT®65 used for?
ASCOGROUT®65 is used as a high-low, non-shrink cementitious precision grout for various applications.
What consistencies can ASCOGROUT®65 be applied in?
ASCOGROUT®65 can be applied in consistencies ranging from flowable to trowelable.
What are the key features of ASCOGROUT® 65?
ASCOGROUT®65 provides high strength, plastic expansion, excellent substrate adhesion, and durability.
How does ASCOGROUT®65 ensure high early strength?
ASCOGROUT®65 ensures high early strength due to its low water demand.
